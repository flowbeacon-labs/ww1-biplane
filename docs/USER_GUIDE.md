# Wings of 1918 — Player Guide

## Day and Evening maps

Choose **Time of day → Day** for bright daylight or **Time of day → Evening** for sunset-colored skies, warm low-angle aircraft and terrain lighting, a visible sun, and runway edge/threshold lights. The choice applies to Flanders, Picardy and Italian Valley, with all four airstrips preserved. Day and Evening can also be combined with Clear, Breezy or Stormy weather. Switching the time does not reset your current mission, and the choice is stored with your pilot career data.

## First Solo Flight: Step-by-step landing tutorial

Choose **Arcade — assisted**, **Clear** weather, **Day**, and preferably the **Curtiss JN-4 Jenny** (lower simulated stall speed) or the Sopwith Camel. Start **First Solo Flight**.

The full mission bar is hidden while flying. A smaller **Landing School** panel is shown for this tutorial only. Tap **HIDE TIPS** to collapse it, or **SHOW TIPS** to reopen it. Press **PAUSE** to return to the mission selector. The panel automatically updates using current aircraft speed, altitude, heading, sink rate and distance to the closest runway.

1. **Take off** — Release BRAKE and hold + POWER. The panel gives an aircraft-specific speed at which to begin pulling back gently on the stick (W or joystick up). Do not pull steeply before reaching it.
2. **Climb** — Build altitude gradually to **80 m**. Hold moderate power and keep the nose only gently elevated.
3. **Turn toward a strip** — After 80 m, the guide selects the nearest marked airstrip and instructs **LEFT** or **RIGHT** turns. Use short A/D inputs or move the joystick left/right briefly, then release it to level the wings. Begin descending slowly toward about **50 m** while still some distance away.
4. **Line up and slow** — Approach the runway *along* its length, not sideways. The tutor displays a target heading (for example, 180° for a southbound approach), a target speed derived from the selected plane and instructions to reduce throttle to roughly 20–35% if too fast. Ease down toward **15–20 m**, then **8–12 m** once above the runway.
5. **Touch down gently** — Keep wings and nose nearly level, speed close to the indicated target and vertical descent gentler than around **3 m/s**. Avoid sideways runway approaches: oblique landings count as **hard landings** even in Arcade mode. Brake after touchdown.

**Typical simulated approach speeds (approximate):** Sopwith Camel around **99 km/h**, Curtiss JN-4 Jenny around **84 km/h**. The instructor displays a dynamic target for the aircraft you select. These are not real aircraft operating speeds.

A safe touchdown on one of the four marked airstrips completes the first mission. A grass landing outside the runway does not award mission landing credit. Even in Arcade mode, a very steep, fast or sideways landing may still be hard.

## Quick start on iPhone
1. Open https://flowbeacon-labs.github.io/ww1-biplane/ in Safari and turn the phone to landscape where practical.
2. Select **First Solo Flight**, **Arcade** handling, **Clear** weather, and the **Sopwith Camel**.
3. Tap **START FLIGHT**. Tap **BRAKE** once to release it.
4. Hold **+ POWER** until the airspeed rises. Move the joystick gently **up** to lift off.
5. Climb toward 80 m (ALTITUDE on the HUD). Use the joystick left/right for banking.
6. Turn back to the runway, lower throttle, and use a gentle descent. Aim for a near-level, low-speed landing.
7. Open **MISSIONS** to pick the next challenge. The next mission starts as a fresh flight.

## Mission objectives
- **First Solo:** Reach 80 m, then land; this is the recommended tutorial.
- **Sky Rings:** Follow the yellow rings in order. The ring course is deliberately off to the sides of the airstrip, so you must take off, turn away from the runway, and intercept the first ring to the east. Cross each near its center and return to the strip for the final score.
- **Reconnaissance:** Fly near the highlighted landmark (within approximately 145 m), then press **PHOTO**. Visit the village, bridge and railway in order and land.
- **Formation:** Fly close enough to the two AI wingmen to accumulate formation time; follow five checkpoints and return.
- **Emergency:** At 75 m the engine is limited. Glide and land carefully.
- **Dogfight:** Approach the moving red practice aircraft, aim ahead, and press **FIRE**; avoid enemy practice shots and return to base after disabling the opponent.

## Finding another airstrip

There are four usable landing strips, including the original home runway:

| Airstrip | Relative location | Runway direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home Airfield | Center of the practice field | North–south |
| Western Meadow Strip | West/southwest of home | North–south |
| Eastern Patrol Strip | East/southeast of home | North–south |
| Northern Farm Strip | North of home | East–west |

Look for pale runway markings, threshold bars, and hangars beside the satellite strips. The always-visible **NEAREST** cue shows the nearest landing field and approximate distance. For mission completion, land within the strip's marked boundaries with a gentle touchdown. Landing outside a runway may be survivable but earns no mission landing credit.

## Display and controls
- **Airspeed:** km/h, simulated.
- **Altitude:** metres above ground.
- **Vertical speed:** positive indicates climb, negative indicates descent.
- **Throttle:** engine power from 0 to 100%.
- **Compass:** current heading.
- **Hull:** health available, reduced during practice combat.
- **Fuel:** limited; reduce throttle and land before it reaches zero.
- **Radar:** shows the current objective position relative to the practice field when detailed statistics are expanded.
- **STATS:** hidden by default to keep the forward view clear. Tap STATS to reveal instrument gauges, telemetry and radar; tap HIDE STATS to remove them again without interrupting the flight.
- **CAMERA:** cycles between chase, closer chase and cockpit-like viewpoint.
- **SOUND OFF/ON:** toggles an original synthesized engine tone. This button is necessary because mobile browsers block unsolicited sound.

## Career and unlocks
Completing missions earns XP and medal stars. Flight rank upgrades and local best scores are saved on the same device. At 100 XP, you can select Sky Blue paint. At 400 XP, Ace Gold paint; at 600 XP, the S.E.5a; and at 1,100 XP, the Fokker D.VII.

**Export/import:** In the mission selection screen, use EXPORT SAVE to download a backup. On a new device use IMPORT SAVE to load the JSON file. Reset Career requires confirmation; export first if you may want to restore progress.

## Troubleshooting
| Issue | Try |
|---|---|
| Aircraft or landscape not visible | Reload; ensure WebGL is enabled and hardware acceleration is available. The game reports a lost graphics context. |
| Plane will not move | Release BRAKE and increase throttle; check that the game is not paused. |
| Plane won't take off | Build speed before pulling up. Try the forgiving Curtiss Jenny or Arcade handling. |
| Plane stalls | Lower the nose and increase power; avoid climbing too steeply. |
| Landing is too hard | Use a shallower descent, lower bank angle, reduce vertical speed, and slow down before touching down. |
| Touch controls don't respond | Use Safari/Chrome in landscape, release any held pointer after an interruption, then restart if necessary. |
| Sound is silent | Tap SOUND ON. Check the device volume and mute settings. |
| Save progress missing | Private browsing or clearing website data may remove localStorage. Use Export Save regularly. |
| Unlocked plane still locked | Check current XP; reload if a new purchase-free unlock doesn't appear after earning points. |
| Slow on mobile | Close other tabs, avoid battery-saving mode, use Clear weather, and reload the game. |

## Limitations
The game is inspired by WWI aviation but is not a historical archive or flight training product. Flight handling, speeds, combat damage and terrain are approximations. No multiplayer, full 3D cockpit instrumentation, real ballistics, or backend storage is included.

## iPhone flight control icons

- **Plus / minus:** hold to increase or reduce throttle. Release your finger to stop adjusting power.
- **Circled P:** toggle wheel brake before takeoff and after landing.
- **Camera:** change chase, close, or cockpit view.
- **Pause bars:** pause and open mission selection.
- **Speaker:** toggle engine audio.
- **Target:** fire in the practice dogfight mission.
- **Camera picture:** take a reconnaissance photograph.
- **Plane / chart icons:** return to aircraft selection or open detailed flight statistics.

Holding the touch controls should not open Safari's text-selection or link context menu. Native menus, inputs and navigation remain interactive.


## Touchscreen laptops and desktop joystick

The circular virtual flight stick is always available in the bottom-left corner while flying, on **Windows touchscreen laptops, desktop computers with a mouse, tablets and phones**. Drag its knob **up or down to pitch** and **left or right to bank**. Release your finger or mouse button to center it. The same game still supports **W/S pitch** and **A/D bank** from the keyboard, with no separate input mode or setup required.

On desktop, the throttle +/- and brake icons are also available along the bottom edge; click or press and hold as appropriate. The joystick and action controls are laid out to avoid covering the main flight view. The direction compass at the upper right names **NORTH, EAST, SOUTH and WEST** (and intermediate directions).

## Low and Normal graphics settings

From the mission/aircraft selection menu, choose **Graphics quality**:

- **Normal** (default): full scenery detail and sharper rendering, appropriate for a laptop with adequate memory.
- **Low**: fewer decorative trees, clouds and landscape objects, lower internal rendering resolution, fewer display frames, and reduced weather effects. Controls, missions, flight handling, four airstrips, compass and runway lights remain available.

Switch quality at any point from the pause menu; the mission and pilot career progress are preserved. The choice is saved automatically. Use Low if your browser slows down, the game is consuming too much memory, or your device gets warm. You can switch back to Normal without resetting.
