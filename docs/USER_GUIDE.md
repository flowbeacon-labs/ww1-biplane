# Wings of 1918 — Player Guide

## Quick start on iPhone
1. Open https://flowbeacon-labs.github.io/ww1-biplane/ in Safari and turn the phone to landscape where practical.
2. Select **First Solo Flight**, **Arcade** handling, **Clear** weather, and the **Sopwith Camel**.
3. Tap **START FLIGHT**. Tap **BRAKE** once to release it.
4. Hold **+ POWER** until the airspeed rises. Move the joystick gently **up** to lift off.
5. Climb toward 80 m (ALTITUDE on the HUD). Use the joystick left/right for banking.
6. Turn back to the runway, lower throttle, and use a gentle descent. Aim for a near-level, low-speed landing.
7. Open **MISSIONS** to pick the next challenge. The next mission starts as a fresh flight.

## Mission objectives
- **First Solo:** Reach 80 m, then land; this is the recommended tutorial.
- **Sky Rings:** Follow the yellow rings in order. The ring course is deliberately off to the sides of the airstrip, so you must take off, turn away from the runway, and intercept the first ring to the east. Cross each near its center and return to the strip for the final score.
- **Reconnaissance:** Fly near the highlighted landmark (within approximately 145 m), then press **PHOTO**. Visit the village, bridge and railway in order and land.
- **Formation:** Fly close enough to the two AI wingmen to accumulate formation time; follow five checkpoints and return.
- **Emergency:** At 75 m the engine is limited. Glide and land carefully.
- **Dogfight:** Approach the moving red practice aircraft, aim ahead, and press **FIRE**; avoid enemy practice shots and return to base after disabling the opponent.

## Display and controls
- **Airspeed:** km/h, simulated.
- **Altitude:** metres above ground.
- **Vertical speed:** positive indicates climb, negative indicates descent.
- **Throttle:** engine power from 0 to 100%.
- **Compass:** current heading.
- **Hull:** health available, reduced during practice combat.
- **Fuel:** limited; reduce throttle and land before it reaches zero.
- **Radar:** shows the current objective position relative to the practice field when detailed statistics are expanded.
- **STATS:** hidden by default to keep the forward view clear. Tap STATS to reveal instrument gauges, telemetry and radar; tap HIDE STATS to remove them again without interrupting the flight.
- **CAMERA:** cycles between chase, closer chase and cockpit-like viewpoint.
- **SOUND OFF/ON:** toggles an original synthesized engine tone. This button is necessary because mobile browsers block unsolicited sound.

## Career and unlocks
Completing missions earns XP and medal stars. Flight rank upgrades and local best scores are saved on the same device. At 100 XP, you can select Sky Blue paint. At 400 XP, Ace Gold paint; at 600 XP, the S.E.5a; and at 1,100 XP, the Fokker D.VII.

**Export/import:** In the mission selection screen, use EXPORT SAVE to download a backup. On a new device use IMPORT SAVE to load the JSON file. Reset Career requires confirmation; export first if you may want to restore progress.

## Troubleshooting
| Issue | Try |
|---|---|
| Aircraft or landscape not visible | Reload; ensure WebGL is enabled and hardware acceleration is available. The game reports a lost graphics context. |
| Plane will not move | Release BRAKE and increase throttle; check that the game is not paused. |
| Plane won't take off | Build speed before pulling up. Try the forgiving Curtiss Jenny or Arcade handling. |
| Plane stalls | Lower the nose and increase power; avoid climbing too steeply. |
| Landing is too hard | Use a shallower descent, lower bank angle, reduce vertical speed, and slow down before touching down. |
| Touch controls don't respond | Use Safari/Chrome in landscape, release any held pointer after an interruption, then restart if necessary. |
| Sound is silent | Tap SOUND ON. Check the device volume and mute settings. |
| Save progress missing | Private browsing or clearing website data may remove localStorage. Use Export Save regularly. |
| Unlocked plane still locked | Check current XP; reload if a new purchase-free unlock doesn't appear after earning points. |
| Slow on mobile | Close other tabs, avoid battery-saving mode, use Clear weather, and reload the game. |

## Limitations
The game is inspired by WWI aviation but is not a historical archive or flight training product. Flight handling, speeds, combat damage and terrain are approximations. No multiplayer, full 3D cockpit instrumentation, real ballistics, or backend storage is included.
