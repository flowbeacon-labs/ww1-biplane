Flight controls

Keyboard: ↑ throttle up, ↓ throttle down, W/S pitch, A/D bank, Q/E rudder, Space brake, C camera, R restart, Esc pause. On iPhone, use the joystick and onscreen controls. Press F to fire and P to take a photo. Progress is saved on this device.

Line up with a marked airstrip and descend slowly; a sideways or oblique touchdown is a hard landing. Arcade mode forgives a somewhat steeper touchdown on a strip and lets the aircraft settle during rollout. Use the MISSIONS button to pause. Select a new mission to restart the flight. All aircraft flight physics and shapes are game approximations, not engineering-grade simulations.
