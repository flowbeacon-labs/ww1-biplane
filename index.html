Flight controls

Keyboard: ↑ throttle up, ↓ throttle down, W/S pitch, A/D bank, Q/E rudder, Space brake, C camera, R restart, Esc pause. On iPhone, hold the onscreen controls.

Fly at a safe speed before pulling back gently. Reduce throttle, level the wings and descend slowly for a successful landing. Flight physics and aircraft shapes are game approximations, not engineering-grade simulations.
